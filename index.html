    How to play

    Keep your cat fed, happy, rested and clean. The bars show how your cat is doing, and time keeps passing even when the page is closed.

    Care

    Feed
    Less hungry and a bit happier, but a little messy.
    Brush
    Cleaner and happier. Some cats don't like it.
    Sleep
    Lots of energy back, but the cat wakes up hungry.
    🩺 Vet
    Shows up when your cat is sick or has sore joints.

    Play

    Play with
    Much happier, but tired, hungry and messy.
    Laser dot
    Catch the red dot as many times as you can.
    Play together
    Two of your cats play; they can become friends, or rivals.

    Cuddle

    Pet
    Happier, but too much petting in a row annoys the cat.
    Belly rub
    Very happy, or a scratch, depending on the cat.
    Meow
    Say hi. A little happier.
